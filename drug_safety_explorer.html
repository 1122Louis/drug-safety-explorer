💊 FDA Drug Safety Explorer

Type a drug to see its real-world side-effect reports from the U.S. FDA — how many, over what years, and which reactions come up most. Add a second drug to compare them.

⚠️ These are reports, not proof. A report does not mean the drug caused the problem, and we don't know how many people took each drug — so these counts can't tell you a true risk rate, and a drug with more reports isn't necessarily more dangerous (it may just be used more). Use them to spot patterns worth asking a doctor or researcher about.
